Media Consent, License and Release

I must sign for myself; a ticket purchaser cannot grant media rights for another adult attendee.

2. Event details and related ticket policy

  • Event hours: 6:00 PM–11:00 PM Eastern Time
  • Check-in opens: 5:30 PM; boarding begins: 6:00 PM; scheduled departure: 7:00 PM
  • Venue: Pier 83, Circle Line, West 42nd Street and 12th Avenue, New York, NY 10036
  • Organizer: Foundation for Community Language and Arts, Inc.

I understand that a customer cancellation received on or before August 31, 2026 is subject to a 50% cancellation penalty, and a customer cancellation received after August 31, 2026 is subject to a 100% cancellation penalty, except where law requires otherwise. Transfer requests must reach admin@foundationclarts.org by 6:00 PM Eastern Time on September 9, 2026 and require written confirmation. Organizer cancellation results in a ticket-price refund; following rescheduling, the purchaser has seven calendar days after notice to elect a refund instead of using the replacement date.
