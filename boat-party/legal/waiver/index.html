Participant Assumption of Risk, Waiver and Release

READ CAREFULLY. THIS DOCUMENT AFFECTS LEGAL RIGHTS. This agreement includes a release of ordinary negligence only to the fullest extent legally permitted. It does not waive gross negligence, recklessness, intentional misconduct, or any liability that cannot legally be waived.

1. Event and participant agreement

This agreement applies to my voluntary participation in the Sunset Boat Party organized by Foundation for Community Language and Arts, Inc. (“Foundation”). I understand that I must sign for myself. A ticket purchaser cannot sign this agreement for another adult attendee.

2. Event details and ticket-policy acknowledgment

  • Date: Friday, September 11, 2026
  • Event hours: 6:00 PM–11:00 PM Eastern Time
  • Check-in opens: 5:30 PM; boarding begins: 6:00 PM; scheduled departure: 7:00 PM
  • Venue: Pier 83, Circle Line, West 42nd Street and 12th Avenue, New York, NY 10036
  • Organizer: Foundation for Community Language and Arts, Inc.
  • Minimum age: 18 at boarding with valid government-issued photo identification; only attendees 21 or older with valid government-issued photo identification may purchase, possess, or consume alcohol

I understand that customer-initiated cancellations are final and non-refundable except where law requires otherwise. No-shows, late arrival, missed boarding, and removal for misconduct are non-refundable except where required by law. Ticket-transfer requests must be received at admin@foundationclarts.org by 6:00 PM Eastern Time on September 9, 2026 and are effective only after written confirmation. If the Foundation cancels the Event, the ticket purchase price is refunded; if it reschedules, the ticket carries over unless the purchaser requests a refund within seven calendar days after notice.

3. Voluntary participation and capacity

I confirm that I am at least 18 years old, legally competent to sign this agreement, and choosing to participate voluntarily. My participation in the Event is voluntary and is not required as a condition of any unrelated service, class, program, employment, or affiliation.

I am responsible for deciding whether my health, mobility, balance, swimming ability, medication use, alcohol use, and physical condition permit safe participation. I may consult a medical professional before attending. I will not participate while impaired in a way that creates a danger to myself or others. I will promptly tell appropriate Event personnel about an emergency or an accommodation request without unnecessarily disclosing sensitive medical details.

4. Inherent and other risks

I understand that a nighttime recreational event aboard a vessel involves known and unknown risks, including:

  • vessel movement, waves, wakes, vibration, sudden changes in speed or direction, and seasickness;
  • slips, trips, falls, stairs, ramps, gangways, ladders, uneven or wet surfaces, low clearances, and moving equipment;
  • boarding, docking, undocking, collision, grounding, mechanical failure, and interaction with other vessels or structures;
  • proximity to deep water, falling overboard, cold-water exposure, drowning, and rescue hazards;
  • changing weather, wind, rain, heat, cold, darkness, reduced visibility, and route or schedule changes;
  • crowds, dancing, loud music, flashing or bright lights, limited space, physical contact, and the conduct of other attendees;
  • food, beverage, allergy, alcohol, intoxication, and impaired-person risks;
  • theft, loss, or damage to personal property;
  • illness, medical emergencies, delayed evacuation, and delayed access to land-based medical care; and
  • transportation, parking, walking, and conditions around the venue before and after the Event.

This list is illustrative and not exhaustive. I may stop participating in an optional activity and seek assistance when reasonably possible.

5. Assumption of inherent risk

Knowing the nature of these risks, I voluntarily assume the inherent and ordinary risks of participating in and being present at the Event, including risks arising from my own condition, decisions, or conduct and risks inherent in vessel travel, crowds, dancing, weather, water, and nightlife activities.

I do not assume, and this agreement does not release, risk or liability that applicable law does not permit me to assume or release.

6. Rules and safety instructions

I will follow lawful instructions from the captain, crew, venue personnel, security personnel, emergency responders, and authorized Foundation representatives. I will comply with the Event Terms and Ticket Policy, including the rules concerning respectful conduct, weapons, illegal substances, smoking and vaping, outside food and alcohol, safety equipment, harassment, fighting, and dangerous behavior.

7. Release, including ordinary negligence only where legally permitted

In consideration of being permitted to participate, I knowingly and voluntarily release and agree not to bring a claim against Foundation for Community Language and Arts, Inc. and its directors, officers, employees, authorized volunteers, and agents (collectively, the “Foundation Released Parties”) for personal injury, illness, death, or property loss arising from my participation in the Event, including a claim based on the ordinary negligence or careless mistake of a Foundation Released Party, but only to the fullest extent that such a release is lawful and enforceable under the law that actually applies.

This release is intended to allocate ordinary recreational risk to the participant only where the law permits that allocation. It does not release or limit liability for gross negligence, recklessness, intentional or willful misconduct, fraud, violation of law, or any other duty, claim, or liability that cannot legally be waived or limited.

Nothing in this agreement is intended to release a vessel owner, master, manager, or agent from liability for personal injury or death caused by negligence or fault where 46 U.S.C. § 30527 makes such a provision void. Nothing in this agreement is intended to exempt an owner or operator of a paid place of amusement or recreation from negligence where New York General Obligations Law § 5-326 applies. Any broader reading is rejected, and the clause must be limited to the maximum lawful scope rather than expanded beyond it.

8. Participant responsibility and limited indemnity

I am responsible for harm, loss, or expense caused by my own negligent, unlawful, intentional, or reckless conduct, or by my material violation of safety instructions or the Event Terms.

To the fullest extent permitted by law, I will indemnify and hold harmless the Foundation Released Parties from a third-party claim, loss, damage, or reasonable cost only to the extent directly caused by my own conduct described above. This is not a broad indemnity for every injury or incident connected with the Event. I do not indemnify a Foundation Released Party or any other person for that person’s own negligence, gross negligence, recklessness, intentional misconduct, violation of law, or any liability that applicable law does not permit to be shifted to me.

9. Emergency assistance

If I cannot consent during an emergency, I authorize Event personnel to contact emergency services and provide reasonable first-response assistance within their training. This is not a promise that medical personnel, equipment, or evacuation will be immediately available.

To the extent permitted by law and not otherwise covered, I am responsible for my own medical, ambulance, evacuation, transportation, and related expenses. This provision does not shift an expense that applicable law requires another responsible party to pay.

10. Third parties

The vessel operator, venue, payment processor, food and beverage providers, security providers, photographers, transportation providers, and other vendors may be independent parties. The Foundation does not represent that it controls every third-party act. Nothing here releases a third party beyond what applicable law and this agreement permit.

11. Governing law and severability

This agreement is governed by New York law, except where another law must apply. Subject to applicable law, disputes will be brought in a state or federal court located in New York County, New York.

If a provision is unlawful or unenforceable, it will be limited or removed to the minimum extent necessary, and the remainder will continue where legally permitted. A court must not rewrite this document to waive a right that cannot legally be waived.

12. Understanding, language, and opportunity to ask questions

Before I sign, the contents, significance, and legal effect of this agreement have been fully explained to me in English or, if needed for my understanding, in my first or native language. I confirm that I understand the material terms and have had an opportunity to ask questions and seek independent advice.

I understand that an electronic signature and related audit record may be used as evidence of my agreement, subject to applicable law. No signature may be pre-checked, inferred from silence, or supplied by another attendee.

13. Participant acknowledgment and signature

By signing, I affirm that:

  • I am 18 years of age or older and legally competent;
  • I am signing for myself and participating voluntarily;
  • I understand the Event’s inherent risks and the legal effect of this document;
  • I understand that the release may include ordinary negligence only to the extent legally permitted and does not release gross negligence, recklessness, intentional misconduct, or non-waivable liability;
  • I agree to the limited indemnity only as stated; and
