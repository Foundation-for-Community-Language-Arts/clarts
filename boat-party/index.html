Important information

Frequently asked questions

What time should I arrive?

Please arrive by 6:00 PM on Friday, September 11, 2026. Boarding closes before the boat leaves, and late guests may not be admitted.

Is there an age requirement?

Yes. Guests must be at least 18 at boarding and present valid government-issued photo ID. Only guests 21 or older may purchase, possess, or consume alcohol.

What should I wear?

Dress to party! Comfortable, weather-appropriate clothing and non-marking shoes are recommended for the boat deck.

Are tickets refundable?

Tickets are non-refundable except where required by law. Transfer requests must be received at admin@foundationclarts.org by 6:00 PM ET on September 9, 2026 and are effective only after written confirmation.

Can I bring outside food or drinks?

No outside food or drinks are permitted. Food and beverages will be available for purchase on board.
