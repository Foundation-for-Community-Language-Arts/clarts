Spanish Language Course

Learn Spanish for Communication, Career, and Culture

Join the programSee all programs

At Foundation for Community Language and Arts, Inc., our Spanish language courses are designed to help students develop practical communication skills in speaking, listening, reading, and writing.

Whether you are learning Spanish for personal growth, work, travel, or community engagement, our classes provide a supportive and interactive learning experience for all levels.

Why Learn Spanish?

Spanish is one of the most widely spoken languages in the world and can help you

  • Communicate with more people
  • Expand career opportunities
  • Travel with confidence
  • Connect with Hispanic cultures and communities

We offer:

  • Beginner Spanish
  • Intermediate Spanish
  • Advanced Spanish

Students progress at their own pace with guidance from experienced instructors.

Choose the class format that fits your schedule:

  • In-person classes
  • Virtual classes
  • Weekday and weekend options

Our Spanish courses focus on:

  • Conversational Spanish
  • Pronunciation and vocabulary
  • Grammar and sentence structure
  • Reading comprehension
  • Writing skills
  • Everyday communication

Students practice real-life situations to build confidence and fluency.

Our instructors create a welcoming environment where students can actively participate, practice speaking, and improve their language skills with confidence.

Who Can Join?

Our Spanish courses are ideal for:

  • Beginners with no prior experience
  • Professionals seeking bilingual skills
  • Students preparing for school or travel
  • Community members interested in learning a new language
  • Anyone who wants to communicate in Spanish
